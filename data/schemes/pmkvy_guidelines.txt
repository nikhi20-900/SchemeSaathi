MINISTRY OF SKILL DEVELOPMENT AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP (MSDE)
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
PRADHAN MANTRI KAUSHAL VIKAS YOJANA (PMKVY 4.0)
OFFICIAL NOTIFICATION AND IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK

SCHEME CODE: IN-PMKVY-001
PORTAL: Skill India Digital - https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in/

1. OBJECTIVES:
Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana is the flagship outcome-based skill training program of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE). The scheme aims to mobilize and empower youth across India by offering industry-relevant skill training, recognized certifications, and pathways to wage employment or self-employment.

2. TARGET BENEFICIARIES & ELIGIBILITY:
- Age Criteria: Candidates must be between 15 and 45 years of age.
- Target Groups: School/college dropouts, unemployed youth, or individuals seeking formal recognition of skills.
- Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
- Identity: Possession of a valid Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-seeded bank account is mandatory.
- Prior Qualification: Varies by job role under the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF levels 1 to 5).

3. KEY PROGRAM TRACKS:
A. Short Term Training (STT): 200 to 600 hours of intensive classroom and hands-on laboratory training aligned with industry standards.
B. Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL): Skill certification for workers with informal prior experience through a 12-hour orientation and assessment process.
C. Special Projects: Customized programs for marginalized tribes, remote Himalayan regions, defense personnel, and inmates.

4. INCENTIVES AND BENEFITS:
- 100% Free Training: Course fee, study materials, and assessment fees are fully subsidized by the Government of India.
- Certification: Globally aligned NSQF certificate with QR-code verification.
- Post-Certification Support: Accidental insurance coverage under PMKVY for 3 years, and placement assistance through Rozgar Melas and digital job exchanges.
