GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA - DEPARTMENT OF BACKWARD CLASSES WELFARE
OFFICIAL CIRCULAR: GUIDELINES FOR POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIPS (OBC)

SCHEME CODE: KA-SCHOLARSHIP-001
PORTAL: State Scholarship Portal (SSP) - https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in/

1. OBJECTIVES:
The primary objective of the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme is to provide substantial financial assistance to Other Backward Classes (OBC), Category-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, and 3B students studying at the post-matriculation or post-secondary stage. This enables talented youths from economically underprivileged backgrounds to complete undergraduate, postgraduate, technical, and professional courses without severe financial distress.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- The candidate must be an Indian citizen and a bona fide permanent resident (domicile) of the State of Karnataka.
- The applicant must belong to recognized Backward Classes categories (Category-1, Category-2A, Category-2B, Category-3A, Category-3B) with valid Caste & Income Certificate issued by the Revenue Department (Nadakacheri RD Number).
- Parental/Family Annual Income Limits:
  * Category-1: Total family annual income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.
  * Category-2A, 2B, 3A, 3B: Total family annual income must not exceed ₹2,50,000/- (under revised welfare norms).
- The student must be admitted into a regular post-matric course (Class 11, 12, ITI, Polytechnic Diploma, Degree, PG, or Professional courses like Engineering, Medicine, Law) in an institution approved by the state regulatory board.
- The student must maintain minimum 75% attendance in the academic term.

3. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE AND ENTITLEMENTS:
- Reimbursement of 100% mandatory tuition fee, admission fee, and examination fee as prescribed by the government fee regulation committee.
- Maintenance allowance:
  * Day scholars: ₹500 to ₹750 per month based on course group.
  * Hostellers: ₹1,000 to ₹1,200 per month for authorized boarders.

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
1. Aadhaar Card of the student linked to an active bank account (NPCI Aadhaar seeding verified).
2. Caste and Income Certificate with 15-digit RD Number (Nadakacheri).
3. Previous examination marks card (SSLC / PUC / Degree marksheet).
4. College Admission Receipt and Study Certificate (Bonafide) with Student UUCMS or Registration Number.
5. Hostel Stay Certificate (if claiming hosteller maintenance allowance).

5. APPLICATION AND VERIFICATION PROCESS:
Applications are accepted online exclusively through the State Scholarship Portal (SSP). Students must complete e-Attestation of documents before final submission. Funds are disbursed directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
